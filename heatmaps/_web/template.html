Court & Ball Position Heatmaps

Source: videoplayback (3).mp4 and videoplayback (4).mp4, aggregated from every training_csv tracking clip extracted from each video (all four tactical categories pooled). Player/ball positions are plotted in the project's 1800×900 cm court-plane coordinate system (see fyp/court.py); missing-detection (0,0) points were dropped.

Note: in videoplayback (4) the ball heatmap sits on the opposite side of the net (y<450) from the player heatmap (y>450), unlike videoplayback (3) where both align on the same side. This points to a per-video court-calibration/orientation difference in the source tracking data, not an error in this aggregation - worth checking against that video's homography corners if you need the two heatmaps spatially consistent.
