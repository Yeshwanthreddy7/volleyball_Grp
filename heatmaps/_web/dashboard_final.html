Filtered to "our team" = the camera-near side, self-consistent clips only. A clip is kept only when the (unfiltered) ball position and the (hard-filtered) player positions agree on which side of the net is near - videoplayback (3) passes 95/108 clips (88%), but videoplayback (4) passes only 5/51 (10%): its per-clip court calibration disagrees with the ball in the large majority of clips, so most of its labelled data had to be dropped rather than trusted. The player heatmap above is therefore backed by just 5 real clips for this video - visually smooth from Gaussian blur, but not a statistically meaningful sample. The real fix is regenerating videoplayback (4)'s training_csv with manual --court-corners instead of --auto-court, not filtering after the fact.
